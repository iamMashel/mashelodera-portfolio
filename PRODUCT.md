# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Founders / teams who need AI work shipped** (the primary audience): they want an
  AI feature, an agent, a RAG system, a dashboard, or an eval/annotation pipeline,
  and they want one person who can both train the model and build the product around
  it. They skim, want proof fast, and reach out via WhatsApp, a call, or email.
- **AI-data companies hiring trainers/evaluators** (Turing/Outlier/iMerit-type): they
  care about QA rigor, RLHF/SFT experience, and reliability.
- **Peers, learners, and the curious**: people who land from LinkedIn/X/Substack,
  want to know who Mashel is, follow the writing, or book a workshop.

Africa-rooted, globally credible: proudly Nairobi-based, working remote worldwide.
Mostly desktop in work hours, a meaningful share on mobile from social. Seconds to
earn the scroll.

## Product Purpose

Turn a skim into a conversation. The site sells a person with a rare combination,
proves it with real numbers, and lowers the friction to "message me" to near zero.
Success = a qualified visitor starts a conversation (WhatsApp / call / email) or
subscribes. Secondary: read and rank well when shared.

## Positioning

The spike: **"I train AI, build with it, and teach it."** Almost nobody combines all
three. Backed by real proof (top 10% QA at Outlier, −25% annotation ambiguity at
Turing, 200+ taught, dual degrees in CS + Molecular Biology, agentic products
shipped). Breadth (AI solutions, AI training, frontend, teaching, growth) is a
service menu, not the headline. The personal "why": *we can all build safe
superintelligence, one piece at a time.*

## Brand Personality

Precise, warm, grounded, optimistic. A scientist who builds and teaches: specific
nouns and verbs, real numbers, no hype. Approachable (WhatsApp-first) without being
unserious. Three words: **precise, warm, credible.**

## Anti-references

- The generic dark dev-portfolio (terminal-green, particle backgrounds, tech-logo soup).
- The editorial-serif "restraint" lane and the beige/cream AI body background.
- Generalist "I do everything AI" framing with no spike; price-competing.
- AI-scaffold tells: eyebrows above every section, 01/02/03 on non-sequences,
  hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids.
- Marketing buzzwords (streamline, empower, supercharge, seamless, world-class).
- Fabricated proof. Real metrics are real; placeholders are clearly marked DRAFT.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't claim.** Every assertion is backed by a metric, a case study, or a
   live link.
2. **Spike first, breadth second.** Lead with the rare combination; let services
   carry the range.
3. **One committed signature.** Clay brand, warm neutrals, deep-drenched bands, one
   characterful display face.
4. **Low-friction trust.** WhatsApp, a call, or email is always one click away.
5. **Human, not a CV.** The "off the clock" world (now, bookshelf, playground, uses)
   makes the person real and followable.
6. **Practice what you preach.** Fast, accessible, responsive, purposeful motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large ≥3:1, verified on both the warm-white
  canvas and the deep-clay drenched bands (cream on espresso-clay).
- Full keyboard operability, visible focus-visible, semantic landmarks/headings.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation; motion never gates content.
- Legible from 320px up; touch targets ≥44px; prose capped ~65–75ch.
